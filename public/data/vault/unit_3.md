#### **3.1 Notes: Periodic Phenomena**
Periodic functions take on various characteristics of a function such as increasing, decreasing, and different concavities across repeated intervals. Recognize that any characteristics found in one period of the function will be in every period of the function.

* **Definition of Periodic Function**: A function $f$ is periodic if there exists a positive constant $P$ such that $f(x + P) = f(x)$ for all $x$ in the domain. The smallest such value $P > 0$ is called the **period length**.
* **Midline**: The horizontal line halfway between the maximum and minimum values:
  $$y = \frac{y_{\max} + y_{\min}}{2}$$
* **Amplitude**: Half the distance between the maximum and minimum values:
  $$A = \frac{y_{\max} - y_{\min}}{2}$$

#### **3.2B Notes: Sine, Cosine, and Tangent on Coordinate Circles**
Each figure gives a circle in the $xy$-plane with center at the origin, radius $r = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2}$, and an angle $\theta$ in standard position.

* **Sine**: $\sin\theta = \frac{y}{r}$
* **Cosine**: $\cos\theta = \frac{x}{r}$
* **Tangent**: $\tan\theta = \frac{y}{x} \quad (x \neq 0)$

For an angle $\theta$ in standard position in the $xy$-plane given in radians and a circle centered at the origin with radius $r$, the coordinates of the point of intersection of the terminal ray and the circle are:
$$(x, y) = (r \cos\theta, r \sin\theta)$$

#### **3.6A Notes: Sinusoidal Function Transformations & Modeling**

General sinusoidal model equation:
$$y = A \sin(B(x - C)) + D \quad \text{or} \quad y = A \cos(B(x - C)) + D$$

* **Amplitude**: $|A|$
* **Period**: $P = \frac{2\pi}{|B|}$
* **Frequency**: $f = \frac{1}{P} = \frac{|B|}{2\pi}$
* **Midline**: $y = D$
* **Maximum Value**: $y_{\max} = D + |A|$
* **Minimum Value**: $y_{\min} = D - |A|$

#### **3.12A Test Prep: Equivalent Representations of Trigonometric Functions**

**Problem**: Which of the following expressions is equivalent to $\frac{1 + \tan^2 x}{\sec x}$?
* Applying Pythagorean Identity $1 + \tan^2 x = \sec^2 x$:
  $$\frac{1 + \tan^2 x}{\sec x} = \frac{\sec^2 x}{\sec x} = \sec x$$

   * (d) The distance between $f(\theta)$ and the pole is increasing or decreasing on the interval $\pi < \theta < 2\pi$? Justify your answer.

   * (e) Distance behavior when $r < 0$ and $r$ is decreasing on $\pi < \theta < \frac{3\pi}{2}$:

   * Find the average rate of change of $f$ between $\theta = \theta_1$ and $\theta = \theta_2$:
