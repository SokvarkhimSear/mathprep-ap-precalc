   \end{array}$$

   * (c) $3 \le t \le 4$: $\text{AROC} = \frac{s(4) - s(3)}{4 - 3} = \frac{7 - 4}{1} = 3\text{ feet/min}$.

6. $f(x) = \sqrt{5x}$ at $x = 1$.

**1.2 Rates of Change (Packet & Notes)**

* **Formula Definition:**
  $$\text{Average Rate of Change} = \frac{\text{Change in Output Values}}{\text{Change in Input Values}} = \frac{\Delta y}{\Delta x} = \frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1} = \text{SLOPE}$$
* **Notes Definition:**
  * A positive rate of change indicates that as one quantity increases, the other quantity **increases**.
  * A negative rate of change indicates that as one quantity increases, the other **decreases**.

**1.3 Rates of Change in Linear and Quadratic Functions (Solutions)**

1. $y = 7x - 10$ on $[-6, 4] \implies \text{AROC} = 7$.
2. $y = 5x + 3$ on $[2, 7] \implies \text{AROC} = 5$.
3. $y = x^2 + 4x - 2$ on $[-3, 2]$:
   $$y(2) = 2^2 + 4(2) - 2 = 10, \quad y(-3) = (-3)^2 + 4(-3) - 2 = -5$$
   $$\text{AROC} = \frac{10 - (-5)}{2 - (-3)} = \frac{15}{5} = 3$$
4. $y = 2x^2 + 2x + 1$ on $[2, 5]$:
   $$y(5) = 2(5)^2 + 2(5) + 1 = 61, \quad y(2) = 2(2)^2 + 2(2) + 1 = 13$$
   $$\text{AROC} = \frac{61 - 13}{5 - 2} = \frac{48}{3} = 16$$

5. Quadratic function $g$ has AROC $= 1$ on $[-3, 0]$ and AROC $= 5$ on $[0, 3]$.
   * The change in AROC over length-3 intervals is $5 - 1 = 4$.
   * On $[3, 6]$, the AROC will be $5 + 4 = 9$.
   * Average rate of change of $g$ on $[-3, 6]$ is $\frac{1 + 5 + 9}{3} = 5$.

6. Quadratic function $g$ has AROC $= 1$ on $[0, 4]$ and AROC $= 7$ on $[4, 8]$.
   * Change in AROC per length-4 interval is $7 - 1 = 6$.
   * On $[8, 12]$ AROC $= 13$; on $[12, 16]$ AROC $= 19$; on $[16, 20]$ AROC $= 25$.

7. **Test Prep Solution:**
   $$\text{Rate of change of } g(x) = \frac{g(x + 1) - g(x)}{(x + 1) - x} = g(x + 1) - g(x) = p(x)$$
   If $p(x) = 2$ (a constant), then $g(x)$ must be quadratic, and its AROC changes at a constant rate.
